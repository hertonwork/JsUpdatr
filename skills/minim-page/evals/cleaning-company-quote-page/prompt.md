Build a one-page website for "Alma Clean", a small commercial cleaning company in Naples, Florida. It needs: a hero with the company name and what they do; a services list (offices, medical offices, retail, post-construction cleanup); a quote estimator where the visitor enters square feet and picks a frequency (weekly / twice a week / nightly) and sees an estimated monthly price update live (use any sensible rate table); a request-a-quote form with name, email, phone, square feet and frequency (a real <form> that POSTs to /quote); an FAQ with four questions using <details>; a footer with the address "Naples, FL" and a phone number placeholder. The page must score 100 in all four Lighthouse categories on mobile and desktop and must not load any external JavaScript or CSS. Dark mode should work automatically. Save it as index.html.
